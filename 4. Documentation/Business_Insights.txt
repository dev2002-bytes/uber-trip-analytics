UBER TRIP ANALYTICS — BUSINESS INSIGHTS

==================================================
1. EXECUTIVE INSIGHTS
==================================================

The dataset contains 103,728 Uber trips generating approximately
$1.55M in trip revenue.

Average trip fare is $14.98, while the average trip distance is
3.36 miles and average trip duration is approximately 16 minutes.

Revenue per mile is approximately $4.45.


==================================================
2. DEMAND INSIGHTS
==================================================

Weekend demand is significantly stronger than weekday demand.

Saturday records approximately 18.7K trips and Sunday records
approximately 19.2K trips, making Sunday the highest-demand day
in the dataset.

Friday has the lowest demand at approximately 9.3K trips.

Hourly analysis shows that trip demand increases substantially
during the daytime and afternoon/evening period compared with
the early-morning hours.


==================================================
3. REVENUE INSIGHTS
==================================================

Total trip revenue is approximately $1.55M.

UberX generates the highest revenue among the vehicle categories,
with approximately $583.9K in trip revenue.

UberX also records the highest trip volume with 38,744 trips.

Average trip fare across the dataset is $14.98.


==================================================
4. VEHICLE INSIGHTS
==================================================

UberX is the highest-volume vehicle category with 38,744 trips.

Vehicle trip volumes:

UberX          38,744
Uber Comfort   17,078
Uber Black     16,710
UberXL         16,698
Uber Green     14,498

UberX therefore represents the largest contributor to overall
trip demand.


==================================================
5. LOCATION INSIGHTS
==================================================

Penn Station/Madison Sq West is the most frequent pickup location
in the dashboard analysis.

Upper East Side North is the most frequent drop-off location.

The longest trip identified in the dashboard is:

Pickup:
Lower East Side

Drop-off:
Crown Heights North

Distance:
144.1 miles

Pickup demand is concentrated among a relatively small number
of high-volume locations.


==================================================
6. PAYMENT INSIGHTS
==================================================

Uber Pay is the dominant payment method.

Approximately:

Uber Pay       67.0%
Cash           32.2%
Amazon Pay      0.6%

Google Pay represents a very small share of trips.

The results indicate that digital payment adoption is substantially
higher than cash usage in this dataset.


==================================================
7. DAY/NIGHT INSIGHTS
==================================================

Day trips account for approximately 72.8% of all trips.

Night trips account for approximately 27.2%.

Therefore, the majority of observed demand occurs during the
daytime period.


==================================================
8. OPERATIONAL RECOMMENDATIONS
==================================================

1. Prioritize vehicle availability during high-demand weekend
   periods, particularly Saturday and Sunday.

2. Focus driver supply around high-volume pickup locations such
   as Penn Station/Madison Sq West.

3. Maintain strong UberX availability because UberX contributes
   the highest trip volume and revenue among the analyzed vehicle
   categories.

4. Prioritize operational planning around daytime and afternoon/
   evening demand peaks.

5. Continue supporting digital payment adoption because Uber Pay
   represents the majority of transactions.

6. Monitor unusually long trips separately because extreme-distance
   trips can materially affect revenue, distance and operational
   efficiency metrics.
